Keen Eye Café ☕

Keen Eye Café is a YouTube channel with relaxing spot-the-difference puzzles: cozy scenes, a timer, and Basil, the café cat, who reveals the answers.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@KeenEyeCafe

Keen Eye Cafe Admin

Keen Eye Cafe Admin is the channel's private publishing tool. It runs only on the channel owner's computer and is used only by the channel owner; there is no public sign-up. Through Google, it accesses only the Keen Eye Café YouTube channel in order to upload the channel's videos, read and reply to comments under them, and show the channel's own statistics.
