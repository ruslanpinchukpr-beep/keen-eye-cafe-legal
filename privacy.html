Privacy Policy

Keen Eye Café and Keen Eye Cafe Admin · effective 18 November 2026

Who we are

Keen Eye Café is a YouTube channel with spot-the-difference puzzles. Keen Eye Cafe Admin (“the app”) is the channel's private publishing tool. It runs only on the channel owner's computer and is used only by the channel owner. There is no public sign-up, and no one else can sign in to it.

Data the app receives from Google

The channel owner signs in with Google to connect the Keen Eye Café YouTube channel. The app uses YouTube API Services only for that channel:

  • Uploading videos (youtube.upload): the channel's own videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails.
  • Managing the channel (youtube.force-ssl): editing the channel's own videos and playlists, reading viewers' public comments under the channel's videos, and replying to them or posting the channel's own comments.
  • Statistics (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly): views, watch time and similar figures for the channel's own videos.

The app does not access email, contacts, files or any other data in the Google account.

Viewers who comment

When you comment under a Keen Eye Café video, the app receives what YouTube makes public with that comment: your channel display name, channel ID, profile picture link, the comment text and its time. If you post your puzzle score, it may appear in a leaderboard comment under a later video, shown with your YouTube display name. The app may remember things you wrote in earlier comments (for example, the name you asked us to call you) so the channel can reply personally. We never ask for private information.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the channel's videos, reply to comments, build leaderboards and understand which videos viewers enjoy. It is stored on the channel owner's computer; the Google access token is stored there too and never published. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Keen Eye Cafe Admin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By commenting on YouTube you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and YouTube's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How to delete your data

Reply to any Keen Eye Café comment, or comment under any of our videos, with “forget me”. Within 30 days we will delete everything the app stored about you, remove you from future leaderboards and confirm it in reply. Deleting a comment on YouTube also removes it from the app the next time comments are refreshed.

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored access token then stops working and is deleted.
